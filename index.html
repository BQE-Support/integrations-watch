Methodology & scope notes

Data source

Every unsolved ticket (New, Open, Pending or On Hold) that is in the Zendesk Integrations Team group, has CORE-Module Integrations, has the CORE-Integrations-Area field set (QBD, QBO, Xero, Office 365, MYOB, DocuSign and so on), or names QuickBooks, QBO, QBD, QBWC, Intuit, Xero or MYOB in its subject. The subject match catches QuickBooks tickets filed under another module or not categorized yet, since agents often set the area field only when they solve. Tickets come from the support team's shared Zendesk store, which reads Zendesk's change feed every few minutes. This page doesn't pull anything itself: it reads that store and the shared Jira store. Flags and hours are worked out in your browser, so they stay current between refreshes.

Integration

  • The CORE-Integrations-Area value when it is set. Otherwise the subject decides: QBO, "QuickBooks Online" or Intuit is QuickBooks Online; QBD, "QuickBooks Desktop", QBWC or "Web Connector" is QuickBooks Desktop; plain "QuickBooks" or "QB" is QuickBooks (version not set).
  • "Other / not set" is a ticket in the Integrations Team group or module with no area and no integration named in the subject.

Setup vs troubleshooting

  • Same rule as the QBD/QBO integration report. A QBD or QBO Troubleshooting SubArea makes it troubleshooting. A setup or integration-request SubArea (consulting request, introduction questions, QuickBooks Desktop setup request) makes it a setup request.
  • With no SubArea, the subject decides: setup, set up, config, connect, onboard, install, activate, initial sync, new integration, integrate, implementation, mapping and similar words make it a setup request. Everything else is troubleshooting.
  • The keyword rule is a best guess, so a troubleshooting ticket that says "connect" in its subject lands in setup. Use the note to record anything misfiled.

With Dev (GAL)

  • A GAL-##### in the subject or in the ticket's GAL field puts a ticket in With Dev; it's left out of every flagged view, since it waits on Development. GAL chips show the issue's Support Ranking, and "Missing SR" when it has none.
  • Dev work closed, ticket still open: the GAL issue is Done in Jira while the Zendesk ticket is still open. These usually need a follow-up with the customer, or the ticket can be solved.

ES Escalations (integrations)

  • Open Jira ES issues with an integration component: QuickBooks Data Integration, Quick Books Record Mapping, QB Desktop Utility, QBD Core Conversion Utility, Xero Data Integration, MYOB Data Integration, Integration, DocuSign Integration or Office365 Email Sync. They are linked to Zendesk through the ES Number field. Reviews there last for the day.

Flag definitions

  • No agent reply yet: open 4+ hours with zero public comment from anyone other than the requester.
  • Awaiting our reply: the requester's comment is the most recent public comment, 4+ hours ago.
  • Heavy back-and-forth: 10+ public comments. Shown for a look, not urgent by itself.
  • Recently checked in: the newest comment is an internal note from the last 24 hours. The ticket leaves the flagged views until something newer happens.

Urgency tiers

  • Needs attention now: flagged, last activity within 48 hours.
  • Aging: flagged, last activity 2–7 days ago.
  • Long-stale backlog: flagged, last activity 7+ days ago.
  • Hours are calendar time, not business hours.

Reviewed checkbox & notes

  • Shared by everyone who can open this board, with who changed them last. They stay on this board; nothing is written to Zendesk or Jira.
  • GAL, setup request and heavy back-and-forth reviews last 7 days; ES reviews last the day; other reviews last until someone rechecks them.

Refresh

  • The page refreshes itself every 15 minutes while it's open and on screen, and right away when you come back to it after that. Ticket changes show up within a few minutes.
  • The trend chart gets one point per hour on weekdays, starting 11/25/2026.
